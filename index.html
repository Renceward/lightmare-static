The Lightmare Before Christmas

2026 Lightmare Status: Hopefully Maybe

After two years away from the show, we’re spending a lot of time asking, “Where did we put that?”, “How does this connect?”, and “What in the world does this thing do?”

Nevertheless, we have officially started putting up the Halloween show!

Our current status is “Hopefully Maybe.” We’re working toward bringing the Lightmare back this year, but a few personal matters could prevent us from completing it, so we can’t promise just yet that the show will go on.

If it does, it may be a little more scaled back than usual, and it may start a little later—but we’re giving it a try. We’ll keep everyone updated as we go!

Halloween.

Our not very scary Halloween show is running now through Halloween night. Enjoy a playlist that with songs for all ages including everything from Ghostbusters to Thriller. Our playlist changes from night to night throughout the Halloween season.

Christmas

Our very merry Christmas Show runs from shortly after Thanksgiving through New Years Eve. Songs include traditional holiday classics as well as contemporary favorites. Just like with Halloween, our playlist changes nightly throughout the holiday season.
