No Shows for 2025

The Lightmare Before Christmas will not be running during the 2025 season for either Halloween or Christmas.

Details for the Curious - below.

Halloween.

Our not very scary Halloween show is running now through Halloween night. Enjoy a playlist that with songs for all ages including everything from Ghostbusters to Thriller. Our playlist changes from night to night throughout the Halloween season.

Christmas

Our very merry Christmas Show runs from shortly after Thanksgiving through New Years Eve. Songs include traditional holiday classics as well as contemporary favorites. Just like with Halloween, our playlist changes nightly throughout the holiday season.

Another year Without the Lights

Hey everyone,

Last year Brian had to have spinal surgery right in the middle of decorating season, causing the show to go dark for 2024. We assured everyone that we would be back in 2025. We were actually working on the show earlier in the year, but.... The best laid plans and all that.... The year Brian is having double knee replacement surgery. It will be done as two separate surgeries, one in August and one in November. This once again puts Brian out of commission for another lightshow season.

We do plan or should I say "hope" to be back in 2026. So we are taking an extended intermission. In the meantime, we appreciate your understanding and support.
